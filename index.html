Product Capabilities

Six Modules. One Integrated Signal.

Ditch disconnected logs and clinical templates. NeuCore integrates telemetry, builders, and client feedback loops into a unified, high-trust system.

1. Assess (ROM Telemetry)

Capture range of motion, pain scores, and joint metrics in under 12 minutes during active session observations.

2. Build (Phase-Gated Programs)

Draft structured rehab exercises, target volume, and sets/reps based on objective joint deficit heuristics.

3. Deliver (Client Workspace)

Distribute daily routines directly to the client's workspace dashboard, complete with clear instruction notes.

4. Track (Symptom Log Loop)

Receive real-time logs of pain thresholds, subjective feedback, and completed volumes after every client session.

5. Report (Timeline Trends)

Review longitudinal tracking charts detailing pain levels and ROM symmetry gains to verify progress milestones.

6. Scale (Clinic/Team Controls)

Deploy to large teams with multi-therapist staff seats, secure data standard configurations, and central billing controls.

Platform Loop

The Connected Rehab Loop

See how NeuCore keeps coaches and clients in a continuous feedback loop from assessment to progress tracking.

1

Coach Assessment & Telemetry

Coach documents joint Range of Motion (ROM), active pain scores, and movement notes in a quick assessment session.

2

Graded Program Design

The assessment findings flow into the program builder, letting coaches prescribe precise sets, reps, and RPE goals.

3

Client Workout Session

Clients access their daily routine via their web dashboard, tracking active volume and viewing instruction notes.

4

Real-Time Feedback Logging

After training, clients log completed sets, RPE, and post-session pain ratings. This provides coaches with instant symptom updates.

5

Longitudinal Progress reports

The logged logs update the coach's charts, allowing them to verify compliance and adjust protocols objectively.
