Product Capabilities

Six Modules. One Integrated Signal.

Ditch disconnected logs and clinical templates. NeuCore integrates telemetry, builders, and client feedback loops into a unified, high-trust system.

1. Coach Dashboard

Manage active client rosters, review daily program compliance signals, and view clinical alerts from a central desktop console.

2. Movement Assessment

Capture active joint Range of Motion (ROM), visual angles, and asymmetry thresholds during scheduled session check-ins.

3. Program Builder

Draft structured rehab routines and volume variables based on joint deficit heuristics, establishing phase-gated progressions.

4. Workout Logging

Deliver assigned routines directly to the client's home portal, complete with video instructions, notes, and set logs.

5. Progress Tracking

Track longitudinal symmetry gains, pain timelines, and subjective load tolerance data logged directly by clients.

6. Clinic & Safety Controls

Deploy administrative staff roles, manage legal consent acceptance workflows, and enforce secure data parameters.
