# The Go-To Therapist Method: Eight Pillars of Clinical Excellence

The following information outlines the 8 Pillars of the 'Go-To' Therapist Method.

## TIER 4: The 8 Pillars of the 'Go-To' Therapist

Each pillar is designed to provide 100% confidence and clarity in a specific stage of the patient's journey.

### Pillar 1: Subjective Assessment

- **Purpose:** To make sense of symptoms by asking high-quality questions to piece together the patient's story and identify their "dream outcome".
- **Connection:** Forms the working hypothesis used to design a meaningful Objective Assessment.
- **Categorisation:** Sense of Symptoms (S.O.S.).

### Pillar 2: Objective Assessment

- **Purpose:** To cross-link movement strategies with the patient's story, identifying which tissues are doing too much work and which are not doing enough.
- **Connection:** Provides the clinical findings needed for an Effective Explanation.
- **Categorisation:** Sense of Symptoms (S.O.S.).

### Pillar 3: Effective Explanation

- **Purpose:** To explain the problem and solution in simple, non-technical terms specific to the patient's situation.
- **Connection:** Builds the trust required for the patient to commit to the Treatment Plan Design.
- **Categorisation:** Buy-in, Adherence, Retention (B.A.R.).

### Pillar 4: Treatment Plan Design

- **Purpose:** To map out the journey using milestones and set realistic expectations regarding timeframes and session counts.
- **Connection:** Guides the specific selection of Hands-on Treatment and rehab exercises.
- **Categorisation:** Buy-in, Adherence, Retention (B.A.R.).

### Pillar 5: Hands-On Treatment

- **Purpose:** To "desensitise" symptoms for a "fast start," reducing the time delay for the patient to feel results.
- **Connection:** Decreases symptoms to allow the patient to engage in Low-Level Rehab.
- **Categorisation:** Dream Outcome, Milestones, Start Fast (D.O.M.S.).

### Pillar 6: Low-Level Rehab

- **Purpose:** To use coordinative exercises that get quick results, ensuring the patient is not solely reliant on manual therapy.
- **Connection:** Prepares the patient's nervous system for Bridging the Gap.
- **Categorisation:** Dream Outcome, Milestones, Start Fast (D.O.M.S.).

### Pillar 7: Bridging the Gap (Low to High-Level Rehab)

- **Purpose:** To transition from conscious "top-down" cues to reflexive "bottom-up" cues, achieving "thoughtless, fearless movement".
- **Connection:** Establishes the foundation of movement quality before adding Strength and Conditioning.
- **Categorisation:** Dream Outcome, Milestones, Start Fast (D.O.M.S.).

### Pillar 8: Strength & Conditioning

- **Purpose:** To build resilience and "buffer capacity," ensuring the patient can withstand real-world external loads and stress.
- **Connection:** Completes the transition to a truly "Resilient Person".
- **Categorisation:** Dream Outcome, Milestones, Start Fast (D.O.M.S.).
