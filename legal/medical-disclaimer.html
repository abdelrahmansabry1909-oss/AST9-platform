Medical Disclaimer

Last Updated: June 2026 · Draft Version

⚠️ CRITICAL MEDICAL WARNING

AST9 does not replace a doctor, physical therapist, emergency care, diagnosis, or medical treatment.

1. Platform Function & Purpose

AST9 is a coach-guided rehab support and tracking platform. The resources, tools, ROM calculators, and movement templates offered are intended solely as tracking utilities and general educational information for coaches and their clients. NeuCore does not make diagnostic claims, clinical assessments, or recovery duration guarantees.

2. Emergency Medical Symptoms

If you have severe pain, numbness, weakness, chest pain, breathing difficulty, trauma, neurological symptoms, or emergency symptoms, seek medical care immediately. Do not delay professional medical consults or disregard advice due to data shown on this tracker.

3. Non-Diagnostic Modeling

The biomechanics telemetry, joint range visualizers, and movement deficit indices generated are strictly for tracking progress and providing insights for physical movement patterns. They are not medical evaluations and must not be used as clinical diagnostic parameters.

4. Consult Your Physician

Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never start a rehabilitation track without confirming with a health specialist that the physical stress is safe for your injury history.
