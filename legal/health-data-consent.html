Health Data Consent

Last Updated: June 2026 · Draft Version

1. What Data is Collected

To enable recovery monitoring and biomechanical observations, NeuCore processes sensitive indicators including:

  • Range of Motion (ROM) degrees and symmetry indexes.
  • Pain locations, scores (1-10), and subjective description logs.
  • Adherence, duration, and intensity ratings (RPE) of assigned routines.
  • Injury history, phase assessments, and general physical goals.

2. Processing Rationale

We process this sensitive data solely to provide the analytics charts, generate customized movement templates, and enable communications between coaches and clients. This data is not shared with public search engines or external brokers.

3. Revocation of Consent

You can revoke your consent to process health and recovery data at any time. However, because this data is core to the platform's features, revoking consent will restrict your access to dashboard statistics, workouts, and reports.

4. Data Custody

Coaches entering health data on behalf of clients must obtain direct consent from the client prior to uploading records to the database. AST9 acts as a processor and assumes coaches hold valid permissions for all client profiles created.
