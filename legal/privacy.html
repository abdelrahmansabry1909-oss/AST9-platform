Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026 · Draft Version

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when registering an account, submitting a contact form, entering client profiles, or uploading workout logs. This data includes names, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical assessment values provided by coaches.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to deliver movement insight reports, manage exercise library assignments, send notifications, and monitor subscription access. We do not sell or lease personal identifiers to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Protection & Security

NeuCore uses standard encryption techniques and database row-level security (RLS) policies to protect personal data from unauthorized access or alteration. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure; users assume responsibility for account access credentials.

4. Health & Biometric Data

To provide program tracking and joint mobility analytics, the platform stores Range of Motion (ROM) data and pain scale values entered by coaches and clients. This data is handled in accordance with the Health Data Consent guidelines.

5. Your Privacy Choices

You can update profile settings and notifications inside the dashboard settings. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated records, please contact your administrator.
