Refund Policy

Last Updated: June 2026 · Draft Version

1. Subscription Tiers

NeuCore offers month-to-month and annual subscription options for professional coaches and clinic administrators. All billing quotas, slots, and intervals are assigned by system administrators.

2. Refund Windows

To ensure satisfaction, subscription purchases are eligible for a full refund if a cancellation request is submitted within 14 calendar days of activation. Requests made after 14 days will be processed for termination at the end of the current billing cycle with no pro-rated refunds.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request cancellation or refund, contact your system administrator or email support. Refund processing typically takes 5-10 business days depending on payment provider policies.
